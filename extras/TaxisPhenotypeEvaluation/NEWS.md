# TaxisPhenotypeEvaluation 1.0.0

Initial official release of the TaxisPhenotypeEvaluation HADES study package.

### Features
* Multi-CDM comparative evaluation of 5 TAXIS-derived clinical phenotypes against comparator cohorts from the OHDSI Phenotype Library.
* Pairwise patient-level cohort overlap calculation with Jaccard similarity indices.
* Mathematical complementary small-cell suppression (< 5 masked to -1) across 2x2 contingency tables.
* Integration with CohortDiagnostics and PheValuator predictive modeling.
* Exact-path allowlist packaging preventing unauthorized file disclosure.
